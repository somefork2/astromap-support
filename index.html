FAQ

I don't know my exact birth time.
Choose “± 15 min”, “± hour” or “I don't know” when entering the birth data. The map then shows bands where each line could be. Planet lines move about 100 km for every 4 minutes of birth time.
Why do I get a different score for the same city as my friend?
Scores are personal: they depend on where your planet lines run. 50 means “average for you across 649 cities”, 85+ is your top few percent.
How accurate are the calculations?
Planet positions are verified against the Swiss Ephemeris to within 4 arcminutes (a few kilometres on the map). Lines use right ascension (in mundo). Above 50° latitude line weights are reduced because all lines converge near the polar circle.
How do I cancel the subscription?
Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Astro Atlas. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid renewal. To restore a purchase on a new device, tap “Restore” on the subscription screen.
Where is my data stored?
Only on your device. See the privacy policy.
